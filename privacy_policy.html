Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 29, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how 3.O Games ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy is designed to help you understand what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can update, manage, export, and delete your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Content you upload: We store the video files, thumbnail images, subtitle files (.vtt, .srt), and movie metadata (titles, descriptions, categories) that you upload to the dashboard.
  • Generated Content: We store AI-generated descriptions created through our integration with OpenAI.
  • App Usage Data: We may collect anonymous information about how you use the app to improve performance and stability.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services (e.g., hosting your videos and enabling playback).
  • To generate creative content (e.g., using movie titles to generate descriptions via OpenAI).
  • To communicate with you about service updates or support.

3. Third-Party Services

We utilize third-party services to facilitate our application's functionality. These parties have their own privacy policies addressing how they use such information.

Google Firebase

We use Firebase for:

  • Authentication: To manage user access (if applicable).
  • Realtime Database: To store metadata about your movies.
  • Cloud Storage: To securely store your video, image, and subtitle files.

For more information, please see Google Firebase Privacy Policy.

OpenAI API

We use OpenAI's API to generate movie descriptions. When you use the "AI Generate" feature, the movie title and year are sent to OpenAI to generate the text. We do not use this data to train OpenAI models.

For more information, please see OpenAI Privacy Policy.

4. Data Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your uploaded content for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. You can delete specific movies, videos, and associated data directly within the dashboard at any time. When you delete a movie, we permanently remove the database entry and all associated files (video, thumbnail, subtitles) from our storage.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
